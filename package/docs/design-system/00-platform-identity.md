## Platform Identity

- **Name**: DGEM (not "DGEM 2.0" in user-facing copy)
- **Full expansion** (first mention only): DGEM (Digital Global Enterprise Model)
- **Font**: Ubuntu (Google Fonts)
- **Visual tone**: Professional, clean, spacious – inspired by enterprise SaaS. Low-contrast neutrals with bold blue and turquoise accent pops.

### Application Modules

All module logos share the **same brand color scheme**: Dark Blue `#121A38` body with Light Blue `#1DB8F2` accent dots. Modules are differentiated by **geometry/shape**, not by color.
